About Research

Ismaïl Saadi

I'm a researcher working where transport, cities and population health meet.

I study how people adapt their travel behaviour to new urban models, such as the 15-minute city, low-traffic neighbourhoods, car-free zones, superblocks and new cycling infrastructure. I also study how those changes shape what people are exposed to: air quality, noise, heat, physical activity, access to green and blue space, and traffic injuries. Together, these exposures affect population health, including morbidity and mortality.

My methods include agent-based simulation, population synthesis, travel behaviour modelling and mobile phone data. I was actively involved in the JIBE and GLASST projects, and I now contribute to DARe.
